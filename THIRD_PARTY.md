## Production tools

- [HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes): code-driven film composition. Repository/component versions and license notices travel with the media source, not copied as a license for the narration.
- [OpenDesign Motion / motion-anything](https://github.com/nexu-io/motion-anything): earlier film generation. Native-video use is recorded separately in the media manifest.
- [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/): generated narration through the configured Sarah voice. A paid/proprietary service, not FOSS. No API credential is distributed.
- [Anatomy](https://skills.wheresryan.sh/anatomy): layout/explanation guidance. Original robot illustrations explain the system; they are not physical CAD or sensor evidence.
- [draw.io](https://github.com/jgraph/drawio-desktop), [Mermaid](https://github.com/mermaid-js/mermaid), and [Excalidraw](https://github.com/excalidraw/excalidraw): editable documentation diagrams. Renderer dependencies have their own notices and pinned lockfile.

## Research materials

The publication includes original analyses, bibliographic links and source manifests. Raw third-party papers, complete web-page captures, caches, vendor clones, credentials, local journals and private execution reports are deliberately excluded. They remain in the local working archive where applicable. Links are citations, not redistribution rights. Synthetic company-policy fixtures and simulation geometry are labeled as original demonstration data.
