# Launch film master v1

## Creative decision
Original evidence-led product-launch film, not a feature slideshow. Near-black/off-white with amber safety signals; large typography and close-ups of genuine native capture. Wistia's product-video guide informed: put product early, answer why/how, show rather than describe, use a small palette, let music set pace. Article read in full: https://wistia.com/blog/product-video-best-practices (Meisha Bochicchio, 2021-10-08). Additional source/license notes arrive in research-script.md. No performance claim borrowed from article.

## Locked narration (continuous Sarah performance)

A robot shouldn't move just because an AI sounds confident.

It should move when the evidence says it can.

Meet Witness Fleet. Evidence before motion.

A blocked corridor. A waiting delivery. Three robots with different jobs, and one question: who has the authority to move?

Witness Fleet connects the answer in FalkorDB. The source, the policy, the owner, the observation, and the command. Language-model suggestions are advisory. They don't get the keys.

Now watch the actual simulation.

The scout inspects. The recovery robot clears the obstruction. Physical observations are retained, not guessed. And the courier still waits.

Because a clear path is not the same as permission.

This recorded run uses an explicit, configured demo-owner release. Then the courier checks the route with fresh evidence of its own, and makes the delivery.

Every completed task leaves a command-bound receipt. If a command's outcome is unknown, the fleet holds. It doesn't guess and send it again.

Seven tasks. One traceable mission.

This is a simulation-first system, not a claim of production robot safety.

Explore the evidence. Try the demo. Read the code.

Witness Fleet. Motion you can account for.

## Picture spine (retime to measured performance)

2. Product name and designed scout/recovery/courier silhouettes, no fake screenshots.
3. Evidence mechanism animation, five source-grounded relationships, FalkorDB name prominent. No claim that SDK output grants authority.

5. Owner gate beat: hold frame from real pause; explicit readable `Configured demo-owner release · recorded run` qualifier. Do not depict authenticated human.
6. Genuine capture courier fresh sensing/delivery, leave purposeful music beat for arrival.
7. Real sanitized receipt IDs/outcomes from manifest. Seven is an actual completed-task count, not animated marketing counter.
8. HOLD contract explanatory visual accurately labeled logic illustration, not a second supposedly recorded incident.
9. Honest scope + invitation, source URL https://github.com/guglxni/witness-fleet.
