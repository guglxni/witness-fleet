## Changes

- Missing projections now say **Unavailable** for dispatch in the overview rather than imply a backend denial.
- Retained projections are clearly labeled cached/stale after offline refresh or interrupted SSE; the cache banner includes revision and world tick, freshness reports stale, and writes remain locked.
- Physical, Policy, Claim, and Freshness are explicitly separate unknown/observed rows. Dispatch is retained as an additional backend gate. Source-record count is not presented as a claim verdict.

- Main reason text is 16 px; gate/status/source metadata is at least 13 px. At 320 px, section headings and long IDs wrap. Long ID controls wrap and table/witness tables remain internally scrollable rather than widening the page.
