Controller/sensor branches must not select outcomes from scenario IDs. Corridor spans x[-7,7], y[-0.7,0.7], bay opening near x0 in north wall. Durable receipts and graph epochs are mandatory. A model is not required to execute this deterministic safe path.

Failure targets: heavy object produces blocked recovery, narrow clearance does not infer courier eligibility from scout fit, occlusion remains unknown and bounded investigation stops; invalid/detached restriction never disappears from authorization.
